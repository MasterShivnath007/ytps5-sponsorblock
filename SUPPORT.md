# Support ytps5

`ytps5` is free, GPL-3.0 licensed, and developed in the open. The public build
has no paywalled feature, no telemetry, and no account requirement. It is
maintained by **master_shivnath**.

If the project saved you time, there are three ways to keep it maintained.

## 1. Star and share

A star helps other PS5 users find the project. A short post or a link from your
own page helps more than a one-off donation.

- [Star the repository](https://github.com/MasterShivnath007/ytps5-sponsorblock)
- [Share the latest release](https://github.com/MasterShivnath007/ytps5-sponsorblock/releases/latest)

## 2. Sponsor the maintenance

Recurring sponsorship pays for firmware maintenance, release builds, test
hardware, documentation, and the unglamorous bug triage.

- **[Sponsor monthly on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/MasterShivnath007)** —
  monthly or one-time support through GitHub Sponsors.
- One-time Razorpay support is temporarily paused while the payment page is
  sanitised. GitHub Sponsors is available.

Sponsorship is optional and does not change the GPL-3.0 license or the public
nature of the code.

## 3. Report bugs and share test results

The most useful non-financial contribution is a clear bug report with logs,
firmware, and the exact app title ID. Use the issue template and attach:

- `/data/ytps5-sponsorblock/relay.log`
- `/data/ytps5-sponsorblock/injector.log`
- `/data/ytps5-sponsorblock/proxy_server.log`

Do not attach app packages, keys, or copyrighted content.

## What your money does not buy

- It does not remove the GPL-3.0 license or make the public code private.
- It does not add telemetry, ads, or a mandatory account.
- It does not change the safety rules: Y2JB apps remain unsupported for
  patching.
